Word Sphere — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Word Sphere ("the app") is a 3D word-search puzzle game developed by Dereje Tesfu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

The short version

Word Sphere has no accounts, no sign-in, and collects no personal data itself. All gameplay data (best times, daily-challenge streaks, settings) is stored only on your device. The only third-party data processing comes from the advertising SDK described below.

Data stored on your device

Game progress — best times, daily-challenge results and streaks, the selected language and theme, and whether the tutorial has been shown — is saved in local app storage on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows occasional interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse, IP-derived location
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

We have configured the app to request non-personalized ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be shown a consent dialog before any ads are loaded, and you can decline. Ads are only loaded when your device is online; the game itself is fully playable offline, in which case no ad data is processed at all.

How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content.

Data we collect directly

None. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK operated by us, and makes no network requests of its own.

Your choices

  • You can reset your device advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date revised before the change takes effect.
